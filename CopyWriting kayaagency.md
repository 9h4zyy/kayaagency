10. FAQs  
- Headline: Frequently Asked Questions  
- What is Kaya Chat?

  Kaya Chat is a live voice party app that lets you join voice rooms, connect with new people, host your own room, send virtual gifts, earn coins, and move up the leaderboards.


- What is the difference between a host and an agent?

  Hosts mainly focus on participating in voice rooms and earning host-related rewards. Agents, on the other hand, recruit and support host teams, invite hosts and sub-agents, and earn eligible commissions and referral rewards.

- How do I become a host?

  Download Kaya Chat, create an account, and complete your profile. Then, go to Profile \> “Become a Host,” finish the real-person verification, tap “Join Agency,” and enter the invitation code provided to you.


- How do I become an agent?

  First, create a Kaya Chat account and copy your Kaya ID. Next, visit the official agent application link and request a verification code. Copy the code from System Notification, enter it on the application page, and then check “Agency Management” in your profile.
