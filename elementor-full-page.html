Build a Team. Support Hosts. Grow Your Agency.

A Kaya Chat agency is a team of hosts managed and supported by an agent. Below is what an agent does.

1 Recruit new hosts and help them join your agency.
2 Support hosts with their daily activities and tasks.
3 Invite sub-agents to grow your agency network.
4 Guide hosts in creating quality content.
5 Help your team understand and follow Kaya Chat's rules.
6 Monitor team activity and eligible commission opportunities.

Ready to Become a Kaya Chat Agent?

Getting started is straightforward. Create your Kaya Chat account, get your Kaya ID, and complete the official agent application process.

Click to enlarge
1

Step 1. Download Kaya Chat

Download Kaya Chat from our official download link and install it

2

Step 2. Create Your Account

Sign up using your Google account or phone number. Google registration is recommended.

3

Step 3. Complete Your Profile

Fill in the required information and make sure your account details are correct.

4

Step 4. Copy Your Kaya ID

Open your Profile or Me page and copy your Kaya ID. You will need it for the application.

5

Step 5. Open the Agent Application Page

Visit the official Kaya Chat agent application page. Enter your agency information and tap Get to request your verification code.

6

Step 6. Retrieve Your Verification Code

  • 1. Open the message menu.
  • 2. Select System Notification.
  • 3. Find the verification message.
7

Step 7. Submit Your Application

Return to the agent application page. Paste your verification code and tap Submit.

8

Step 8. Check Your Agency Information

Open your Profile page and select Agency Management to view your agency information.

User Role

Kaya Chat Host

Main Role

Create voice rooms and facilitate engaging conversations with other participants.

Income Opportunities

Earn through chatting activity rewards, the 7-Day Income Task, virtual gifts, referral incentives, and leaderboard bonuses.

Ideal for

Individuals who enjoy interacting with others, joining live voice sessions

Leadership Role

Kaya Chat Agent

Main Role

Recruit, coordinate, and manage a team of hosts and sub-agents.

Income Opportunities

Generate income through agency commissions, game-related rewards, referral incentives, and agency bonuses

Ideal for

Individuals interested in team management, host development, recruitment
